## Publication barrier

The job builds an immutable GCS generation, then copies and verifies required R2 artifacts **before** D1 visibility can advance. Required keys: the scan's `meta.json`, `age.json`, the current generation's `attr.tsv` and index sidecar, plus the rules registry. Lifecycle remains optional when its upstream pull fails; files that exist are copied.

`dt-cloud publish-r2 -R KEY` checks source membership before upload and destination size/md5 after upload or skip. A required same-size destination with an unknown checksum is re-copied, so retries can repair it rather than failing forever after a skip. Missing credentials, failed copy or failed verification records `publish-r2` failure and terminates before index-sync. The previous scan remains visible. Source files may exist privately before this barrier; that is not a published scan.

Afterward static-name append, interval-store append and `index-sync -r` stage rows concurrently. Both append workers and row upload finish before `index-sync -p` verifies and exposes the generation (full-sync fallback on pointer refusal). Static/interval failures retain their explicit refusal/per-scan fallback; they cannot bypass the R2 gate.

Repair copies with `publish-r2 -S -a` plus the required-key checks, then resume append/row/pointer stages for the same immutable generation. Do not expose incomplete files, overwrite an existing generation or run index GC between staging rows and flipping pointers.

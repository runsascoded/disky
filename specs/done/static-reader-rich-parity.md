# Static reader parity across dispatch modes

Implemented in the shared reader and site. A compacted generation can select a rollup where its predecessor selected matching roots: row-group bounds and the historical roster affect dispatch. This must preserve the byte/object answer and all fetched rich detail. Complete JSON equality also depends on bounded refinement, lookup annotations and generation-wide root-count bounds.

`foldRoots` now retains owner mixtures for its bounded drawn/listed nodes and the answered directory, including contributions from roots too small to draw. `nestRollup` propagates those mixtures into enclosing aggregates without adding their byte/object totals again. Root point lookups propagate their date/read/class display detail into drawn ancestors, as the plain reader does. No additional storage reads or full ancestor forest are retained.

An unexpanded rollup has no owner detail. Its `ou` field explicitly counts unread ownership bytes; the known unowned remainder is `b − Σ us − ou`. The site distinguishes these states in the map, owner bars and coverage note. Live assignment overlays move assigned bytes out of `ou`, including ancestor totals; local filtering recomputes `ou` from retained children. Unknown ownership is not a user or an unowned assignment target.

A filtered diff previously treated a missing drawn ancestor of matching roots as absent unless the ancestor itself was under a matching root. A small existing subtree drawn only on the other date could therefore appear newly added. The reader now sums live matching roots into only the requested missing ancestors. Requests below a matching directory retain the ordinary point lookup. Zero-byte object counts are preserved. Both folded and unfolded static paths use this correction; it does not increase storage read budgets. Filtered response cache version is 10.

Validation includes failing-before/passing-after owner, rich-detail and ancestor regressions, folded/unfolded diff parity over the existing fixture matrix, explicit unknown ownership and assignment-overlay tests, client/Functions type checks, the complete site suite and browser-rendered known/unknown owner states. A separate private generation-pinned real-data audit retains complete bodies and classifies every remaining difference. Passing selected queries does not independently certify complete historical index contents or authorize predecessor deletion; forwarding records and provenance remain subject to the retention audit.
